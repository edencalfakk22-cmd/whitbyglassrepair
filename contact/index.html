Where we work in Whitby

From Downtown Whitby, Brooklin, Port Whitby, Whitby Shores, Pringle Creek, Rolling Acres, Williamsburg, Taunton North, Blue Grass Meadows, Lynde Creek, West Lynde and Werden's Plan, we cover every Whitby neighbourhood — plus the surrounding Durham communities.

Storefront glass at the four corners and along Brock Street. Downtown Whitby carries more than 450 businesses around the four corners at Dundas and Brock, and much of that frontage is old: shallow retail bays in buildings that predate anyone's memory of them, some inside the Werden's Plan Heritage Conservation District, with big single-glazed lites sitting at sidewalk level in frames that have been moving for a century. Delivery dollies, patio furniture, snow shovels and buildings that are no longer square all take their turn. We measure the opening as it sits today rather than trusting an original drawing, and every entrance lite goes back in tempered or laminated safety glass.

Brooklin's Baldwin Street heritage frontages. Downtown Brooklin has been a designated Heritage Conservation District under the Ontario Heritage Act since 2008, and the village dates to 1840. Half the businesses on Baldwin Street trade out of converted Victorian houses, which means wood stops, putty-glazed sash and pane sizes that no supplier holds in stock. That work is measured to the millimetre and matched for thickness and tint, because on a heritage street the whole block reads as one frontage and a mismatched lite is obvious from across the road.
