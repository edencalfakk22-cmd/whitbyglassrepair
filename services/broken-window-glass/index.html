FAQs

Broken and Smashed Window Glass — your questions

My window cracked overnight and nothing hit it — what happened?

Almost certainly thermal stress. On a cold, bright Whitby morning the middle of the pane heats while the edges stay at outdoor temperature, and ordinary annealed glass splits under the difference. The giveaway is a single wandering crack starting at the edge rather than a star or an impact point. It is a glass problem, not something you did.

Can a cracked pane be repaired instead of replaced?

No. A crack in a window pane is permanent and keeps travelling with every temperature swing, and a Durham winter gives it plenty of those between November and March. Tape and resin hold nothing structurally. The safe and permanent fix is replacing the glass, which is usually a single visit once we have the measurement.

Do you replace just the glass or the whole window?

Just the glass, whenever the frame and sash are sound — which is most of the time in Whitby's newer subdivisions. Replacing the pane alone is faster, cleaner and leaves your existing frame, finish and hardware in place. If we find rot, a failed frame or a sash that will not hold glass safely, we tell you before we order anything.
