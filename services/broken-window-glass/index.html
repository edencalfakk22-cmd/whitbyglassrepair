Most broken windows in Whitby were not broken by anybody. A crack that appears overnight on a bright minus-fifteen morning, running as one wandering line out of the edge of the pane rather than radiating from a point, is thermal stress: the middle of the glass warming in low winter sun while the edges stay locked in a frame at outdoor temperature. Ordinary annealed glass splits under that difference, and Durham hands it dozens of freeze-thaw swings between November and March. Stones off a mower, a ladder, wind-borne debris off the lake and the occasional hockey puck cover the rest.

Whichever it was, the fix is the same and it is not tape. A crack in a pane keeps travelling with every temperature swing, so the glass gets replaced, not patched. In the subdivisions that filled Rolling Acres, Taunton North and Williamsburg the sash and frame are almost always still sound, which means only the glass comes out and the window looks untouched when we leave. We measure the rebate on site, put back the thickness and glass type that opening genuinely needs, vacuum the pocket, and go through the lawn, the window well and the flower beds for fragments before we pack up.

FAQs

Broken and Smashed Window Glass — your questions

Nothing hit my window and it cracked anyway — is that normal here?

It is one of the most common calls we get between November and March. Low winter sun heats the centre of a south-facing pane while its edges stay frozen in the frame, and annealed glass splits under the stress. The tell is a single curved line starting at the edge, with no impact point. Tinted, filmed and reflective glass is the most vulnerable, and so is any pane with a heater or a display light blowing across it.

Do I need the whole window replaced or just the pane?

Just the pane, in the large majority of Whitby homes. The sash, the frame, the hardware and the finish are usually fine — only the glass failed. Replacing the pane alone is quicker, cheaper and leaves the window looking as it did. We only raise a full replacement if we find rot, a racked sash or a frame that will not hold glass safely, and we raise it before ordering anything.

How long can I leave a cracked pane before doing something about it?

A hairline that has not gone through is not an overnight emergency, but it is not stable either — every cold snap moves it further and eventually it goes right through, usually at the least convenient moment. If the crack has already gone through, or the pane is a ground-floor opening on the street side, treat it as urgent and call us to secure it.

Will there be glass left in the garden afterwards?

Not if we can help it. Fragments travel much further than people expect — into window wells, into mulch, into the grass a good distance from the wall. We vacuum out the glazing pocket, sweep hard surfaces, go over the beds and the well, and the broken glass leaves with us rather than going into your bin.
