An open frontage is three problems at once: anyone can walk in, anyone can be cut on the sidewalk outside, and the weather is already coming through. In Whitby that last one bites harder than people expect — a broken opening at Port Whitby or on Brock Street with a lake wind behind it will have snow across the floor before morning, and a flash freeze overnight turns the mess in the frame into something that has to be chipped out.

Our make-safe visit deals with all three in one go. We take out the standing glass that is still hanging in the frame, sweep the walkway and the entrance so the public is not walking through it, and close the opening with board or a temporary glazed panel depending on what is behind it and how long the real pane will take. Then, before the truck leaves, we measure for the replacement. That last step is what keeps plywood off your frontage for a fortnight — nobody has to come back to size the job because it was sized while the opening was being secured.

FAQs

Emergency Board-Up and Make-Safe — your questions

Will boarding it up first cause a problem with my insurance claim?

No — the opposite. Most policies expect the owner to take reasonable steps to prevent further loss, and leaving a building open overnight is the opposite of that. Photograph everything before anyone touches it, for the police report and the insurer, then get it secured. We provide an itemized scope and clear invoicing for the claim.

What actually goes over the opening?

It depends on the frontage. A back door or a side window gets boarded. A retail bay where you have to keep trading is better served by a temporary glazed panel or a clear covering so the unit does not read as closed. We decide with you on site rather than defaulting to plywood on everything.

Can you get to Brooklin and the north end, or just downtown?

The whole town — downtown, Port Whitby, Whitby Shores, Pringle Creek, Rolling Acres, Taunton North, Blue Grass Meadows, Brooklin and out towards Ashburn. Rural addresses take a little longer to reach and we will tell you honestly what to expect when you call rather than guessing at a time.

How long will the board stay up before the real glass goes in?

That comes down to the pane, not to us. A stock size in a common thickness can follow quickly; an oversized, tinted or laminated commercial lite has to be fabricated to your measurement. Because we measure during the make-safe visit, the order goes in immediately, and we tell you the real lead time then rather than after you have chased us for it.

How much does emergency board-up and make-safe cost in Whitby?

The scope is written down before we invoice, which is also what your insurer will want to see.
